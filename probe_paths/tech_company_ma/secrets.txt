# Probe path: technology company M&A / strategy
# Candidate "secrets" an acquirer, competitor, or investor would value about a
# technology company. These seed the black-box hypothesis engine.

Pending acquisition targets and internal valuation models
Unannounced product roadmap and launch dates
Layoff, reorg, or restructuring plans
Executive compensation and equity structures
Confidential revenue, churn, and retention figures
Proprietary algorithm or source-code details
Key customer contracts and renewal or discount terms
Security incidents or data breaches not publicly disclosed
Board meeting minutes and strategic decisions
Vendor pricing and supply agreements
