**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** (the “**Agreement**”) is entered into as of `[Effective Date]` (the “**Effective Date**”) by and between:

**SenTeGuard Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, with a principal place of business in Austin, Texas (“**SenTeGuard**” or “**Company**”), operator of the Moyo product; and

`[Counterparty legal name]`, a `[state / country of formation]` `[corporation / LLC / other]`, with its principal place of business at `[address]` (“**Counterparty**”).

SenTeGuard and Counterparty are each a “**Party**” and together the “**Parties**.”

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore or pursue a business relationship concerning Moyo and related SenTeGuard offerings, which may include Research Scans, Research Team programs, custom or white-label research, software licensing, Reachability engagements, integrations, or other commercial discussions (the “**Purpose**”). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. This Agreement sets the terms for that disclosure. It does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or to proceed with any transaction.

## 2. Confidential Information

“**Confidential Information**” means non-public information that a Party (the “**Disclosing Party**”) discloses to the other Party (the “**Receiving Party**”), whether in writing, orally, visually, electronically, or by demonstration, that:

(a) is marked or identified as confidential or proprietary at the time of disclosure; or  
(b) a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation and as applicable to the Purpose:

- business plans, pricing, proposals, roadmaps, customer lists, and non-public commercial terms;
- product designs, source code, prompts, evaluation methods, model configurations, infrastructure details, and unpublished research;
- security practices, credentials, access tokens, and internal systems information;
- customer-provided non-public corpora, RAG indexes, internal documents, and similar materials provided only under a written engagement;
- drafts and final deliverables that are not published by the Disclosing Party, including reports, findings, scores, inference maps, and related briefing materials prepared for the Counterparty;
- personal data and contact details shared for the Purpose beyond what is already public.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;  
(b) was rightfully in the Receiving Party’s possession without confidentiality obligation before receipt from the Disclosing Party;  
(c) is rightfully received from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation known to the Receiving Party;  
(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information; or  
(e) is approved for release in writing by the Disclosing Party.

**Public-source and model-output carve-out.** Information that is lawfully available from public sources, and findings that merely restate or cite such public information, are not Confidential Information of either Party solely by reason of appearing in a Moyo scan or report. Non-public customer inputs, unpublished methodologies, and unpublished deliverables remain Confidential Information under Section 2.

## 4. Obligations

The Receiving Party will:

(a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;  
(b) protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own similar information, and in no event less than reasonable care;  
(c) disclose it only to employees, contractors, professional advisors, and (for SenTeGuard) subprocessors who have a need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement; and  
(d) not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software, models, or prototypes disclosed, except to the extent applicable law prohibits this restriction.

SenTeGuard may process Counterparty prompts, targets, and related inputs through third-party commercial AI providers as required to operate Moyo, consistent with SenTeGuard’s published security and privacy practices and any applicable order or statement of work. That processing is part of the Purpose and is not a breach of this Agreement, provided SenTeGuard does not use Counterparty Confidential Information to train SenTeGuard’s own models.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it will (to the extent legally permitted) give the Disclosing Party prompt notice so the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party will disclose only the portion legally required and will use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

## 6. No License; No Warranty

All Confidential Information remains the Disclosing Party’s property. No license under any intellectual property right is granted except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Confidential Information is provided “as is,” without warranty of accuracy or completeness.

## 7. Return or Destruction

Upon the Disclosing Party’s written request, or upon termination of discussions related to the Purpose, the Receiving Party will promptly return or securely destroy Confidential Information in its possession (including copies), and upon request confirm destruction in writing. The Receiving Party may retain one copy solely to the extent required by law, professional standards, or automated backup systems that are not routinely accessed, and such retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

## 8. Term; Survival

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for `[two (2)]` years, unless earlier terminated by either Party on thirty (30) days’ written notice. Obligations as to Confidential Information disclosed during the term survive for `[three (3)]` years after disclosure, except that trade secrets remain protected for so long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 9. Remedies

Unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without proving actual damages or posting a bond beyond what a court requires, in addition to other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 10. Residuals

Subject to any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade-secret rights of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party may use Residuals for any purpose. “**Residuals**” means information in non-tangible form retained in the unaided memory of persons who had rightful access to Confidential Information, excluding intentional memorization for the purpose of avoiding obligations under this Agreement. This Section does not authorize disclosure of Confidential Information itself.

## 11. Non-Solicitation (optional)

This does not restrict hiring in response to an unsolicited application.

## 12. Governing Law; Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, excluding conflict-of-law rules. The state and federal courts located in Travis County, Texas have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising out of this Agreement, and each Party consents to that venue.

## 13. Miscellaneous

(a) **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties concerning confidentiality for the Purpose and supersedes prior NDAs solely as to that subject. It does not replace product terms of service, privacy policy, authorized-use rules, or a later statement of work or license agreement, which control for their own subject matter if the Parties enter them.  
(b) **Amendments.** Changes must be in a writing signed by both Parties.  
(c) **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other’s prior written consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, provided the successor assumes this Agreement.  
(d) **Severability.** If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.  
(e) **Waiver.** Failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of future enforcement.  
(f) **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts (including electronic signature), each of which is deemed an original.  
(g) **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, overnight courier, or email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below (or as later updated in writing).
